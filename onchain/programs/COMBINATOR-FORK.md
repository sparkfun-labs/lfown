# Forked from zcombinatorio/programs

`amm`, `vault` and `futarchy` are copied from https://github.com/zcombinatorio/programs
at commit `9158dc8` and are licensed under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0
(`LICENSE-AGPL-3.0`), as the originals are. Changes made here are published in this
repository under the same licence. `lfown_raise` is original to LFOwn.

Upstream ran a DAO's money through keys: its treasury and mint sat in Squads multisigs
whose members were the protocol's own keys, a winning proposal was carried out by hand,
and the pool's liquidity was moved in and out of each proposal's markets by a server key.
This fork puts every one of those under the program.

## Changes from upstream

**Identity**
- Program IDs replaced with LFOwn's own (`declare_id!`, `Anchor.toml`).
- `amm` `FEE_AUTHORITY` replaced with an LFOwn key. On localnet and devnet these are
  throwaway keys from `onchain/.keys/`; mainnet keys are generated by the operator and never
  committed.

**Step 1 — the treasury and the mint answer to the market** (`futarchy`)
- Squads, the protocol multisig keys, child DAOs and historical imports are removed.
- The DAO's treasury and mint authority are PDAs (`treasury`, `mint_authority`). A DAO can
  only be opened over a token whose mint authority it already holds.
- Each option but the status quo carries up to four actions (`Transfer` from the
  treasury, `MintTo`), fixed before the markets open (`set_option_actions`).
- `execute_transfer` / `execute_mint`: anyone, once, and only for the option that won.

**Step 2 — the pool's liquidity and fees answer to the program** (`futarchy`)
- The DAO's Meteora DAMM v2 position NFT is held by a PDA (`liquidity`) and attached once
  (`attach_position`).
- `prepare_proposal_liquidity` takes `withdrawal_bps` of the position out; `launch_proposal`
  funds the markets from it and makes the PDA their liquidity provider; `redeem_liquidity`
  (anyone) brings it home; `return_liquidity` (anyone) adds it back to the pool. One proposal
  holds the liquidity at a time.
- `claim_pool_fees` (anyone) claims the position's fees and splits them 50/50 between the
  DAO treasury and LFOwn.
- DAMM v2 is called through its IDL (`idls/cp_amm.json`, `declare_program!`); none of
  Meteora's source is used — it is under a noncommercial licence. The liquidity arithmetic
  (`futarchy/src/liquidity.rs`) is written from the standard concentrated-liquidity formulas.

**`amm`**
- `remove_liquidity` refuses while the pool is still trading: upstream let the liquidity
  provider drain a live proposal's market.
