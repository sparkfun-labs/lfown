Where the money goes

01 · Every trade

Memes pay the DAO

Every meme launched here charges 2.5% per trade. After Meteora's cut, half of it is paid straight into the LFOwn DAO treasury, on every coin, for as long as it trades — on its curve and after it graduates.

02 · The treasury

Held on chain

The treasury is the DAO's own account on MetaDAO. It receives fees in the ownership coins the memes are paired with, so it grows into a basket of the ecosystem it serves.

03 · Futarchy

Markets decide

Spending it takes a proposal on MetaDAO. Traders bet on the token's price with and without it, and the market that expects more value wins. No committee, no multisig vote.
