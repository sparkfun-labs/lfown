Design system

Everything LFOwn is drawn with. The colours below are read out of /assets/lfown.css as the page loads rather than written down here, so this sheet cannot quietly fall out of step with the site — flip the theme and watch them change.

Colour 01

Two roles do the work: ink on paper, and one red that means act or attend. Nothing else is coloured.

Red is never decoration. It marks the thing you can press, the figure that should worry you, and the current page — so a screen with three red things has three claims on your attention, and that is a design bug. The trade panel earns exactly one: price impact.

Edges 03

A 1.5px border and a hard offset shadow. No blur, no radius, no gradients on anything you can click.

Depth is a fixed offset, not a light source. 5px ink is a control, 6px red is a card lifting under the cursor, 10px red is the one panel on a page that matters most. Pressing a button moves it 3px into its own shadow.
