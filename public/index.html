The idea

“Solana will win paired memecoins” Vibhu — 6 Sep 2026 ↗

His argument: the chain wins this meta if the community leans into its structural advantages — more assets with more liquidity, pairing not only against stocks but against other tokens (BTC, ZEC, AI coins, prediction positions… MetaDAO tokens included), more builders running experiments, and a market structure that lets coins sustain momentum instead of getting thrashed by arb.

LFOwn is that argument turned into a product. Pick an ownership coin. Launch a meme against it. The pool's reserve asset stops being a bare ticker and becomes a coin with a treasury behind it.

The gap

$RAWRFlagged
$AVICIFlagged

StonkFun's risk engine doesn't recognise the futarchy mint mechanism behind ownership coins, so it refuses them as a quote token.

  • Mint authority revoked — fail. The futarchy mint reads as an open faucet: “can still mint unlimited new supply”.
  • Liquidity locked — fail. 0% of LP measured as burned or locked, against a 50% minimum.
  • No flagged risks — fail. Third-party flags pile on the thin liquidity typical of a futard.io launch.
